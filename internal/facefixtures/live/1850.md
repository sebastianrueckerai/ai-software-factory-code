One face for every card: the design philosophy on every card kind

The design philosophy exists, is decided, and is confirmed; it must now hold on EVERY kind of card, not only the case rows. The full plan is docs/plans/2026-11-13-one-face-every-card.md in the design repository (ai-software-factory, commit 81d4b73); it is this issue's spec.
What it demands, in short:
- Every asking face (merge, resource, escalation, plan) renders ONE decision at a time in the five-part shape: the ask with numbers, the why, what the call needs, the recommendation with its source, premade answers as buttons with one-line effects, exactly one fund control and one note box, and one send applies all three. The case face's decide block (board.html case-decide) is the shared partial.
- Plain cards whisper: spend muted until 80 percent, colour never alone, normal states at low weight.
- The plan card is audited against what #1837's view-rebuild card lands; the audit closes what remains.
- A bound ask in the chat answers by its button alone; a typed message resolves nothing while it stands open.
- One conformance test renders every card kind from fixtures and pins the face contract, so no new kind drifts.
Confirmed decisions stand unchanged: C42 (no confirmation lines), C44 (the amber band and carousel), C45 (criterion rows). The criteria card needs no rework.
Sequencing: the plan's task 5 runs after #1837's cards (t-b37303f8df4c and its siblings) merge; the rest can run beside them. The reader every face is written for: a human who dropped in on a project they forgot about.
