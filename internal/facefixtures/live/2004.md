The spec of the whole feature:
Spec: A card's first session is priced on its own task's recorded openings, never on the project's newest, so a fresh card with a default budget can afford to run; a budget-floor case asks for exactly the opening price SessionFunded demands (an accepted fund of the asked amount clears the floor); and a fund press on a never-ran card is never weighed by the bought-nothing guard, so a card can be funded again after its first euro. The nine stuck floor cases unblock on deploy: the first-draw cards fund to their printed need, and the guard-dead pair accepts a fund again.
Glossary:
- ReadOpening: internal/policy's read (internal/policy/holds.go) of a task's newest session openings from the ledger, the basis of a session's price
- opening ledger: the ledger rows of kind 'model' whose per-session first calls and opening calls ReadOpening windows
- SessionFunded: internal/factoryd's floor every session start passes — '' when the free headroom funds the opening calls, else a refusal naming the need
- needOf: internal/factoryd's priced need of a session's opening, the larger of the opening hold and the policy session floor of the model's rung
- floor case (CaseBudgetFloor): the case a card trips when its money cannot fund a session's opening; its Money.NeedEUR is the amount the board's Fund asks
- fundFor/FundRaise: the cent-rounded raise that lifts a session ceiling to a need, read off the card's typed figures
- boughtNothing: the fund guard in internal/factoryd/actions.go that refuses a draw after which no push, revision, head, checks or findings changed
- fund press: a person's Fund answer, recorded as a rung-3 pool draw (RecordGrant, internal/factoryd/act.go) on the card's events
